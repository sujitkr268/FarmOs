# FarmOS 🌾
## Connecting Every Harvest to Its Best Opportunity

FarmOS is a modular smart agriculture platform designed to connect farmers, harvests, markets, buyers, and agricultural services through a single digital ecosystem.

The platform focuses on helping farmers make better-informed decisions by combining agricultural data, market information, weather intelligence, digital services, and AI-assisted decision support.

FarmOS is designed with an extensible architecture so additional services such as payments, logistics, notifications, analytics, and other agricultural services can be integrated without changing the core platform concept.
